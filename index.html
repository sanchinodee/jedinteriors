About JED Interiors

Experienced Tradesmen. Professional Results.

JED Interiors Ltd is a painting and plastering company based in Waikato, providing professional services throughout Waikato and Central Otago.

Our director, Jed, is an experienced painter and plasterer with 20 years of local and overseas experience working on residential and commercial new builds and renovations.

We focus on delivering high-quality workmanship while maintaining efficient project completion times.
